吃什喵EatMeowever

不知道吃什么？Can't decide where to eat?

让猫咪帮你印一张点菜单，从附近的餐厅里随机挑一家。打开就能玩，不用注册。Let the cat print you an order ticket for a random place nearby. Open it and play, no sign-up.

去玩Try it

怎么玩How it works

  • 一键出单One tap, one ticket 猫咪打印机随机印出一家附近的店；不满意就换一张，满意就盖章。The printer cat prints a random place nearby. Not feeling it? Print another. Like it? Stamp it.
  • 想吃什么自己挑Pick what you're into 吃的、喝的、甜点，菜系和距离都能筛。Food, drinks or sweets, by cuisine and distance.
  • 滑一滑就决定Swipe to decide 左滑丢掉，右滑看详细，一键打开地图。Swipe left to toss, right for details, then open it in Maps.
  • 不用账号No account 选一个加州城市，或用你的位置。位置只留在你的设备，什么都不存。Pick a California city or use your location. It stays on your device; nothing is saved.

数据与开源Data and open source

  • 店家数据来自Places come from the Overture Maps Foundation 的 Places 数据集。Places dataset.
  • 目前只有加州的店。California only, for now.
  • 代码以 MIT 授权开源。The code is open source under the MIT License.
